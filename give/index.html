Fund the table. Keep a coach in the room.

Root to Rise is a small nonprofit doing unglamorous work: breakfast, packed meals, coaching, job kits, and the training that makes more coaches. Your gift is the whole budget. Root to Rise is a program of Inspiration Is Everywhere Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 82-5186366); gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Give without a card

We collect food, work gear, and the unglamorous things that make a week possible. Drop off can be arranged through the contact form.

  • — Shelf-stable breakfast foods and coffee
  • — Grocery store gift cards
  • — Work boots, interview clothes, prepaid phones
  • — Bus passes and gas cards for Mashpee–Falmouth trips
  • — Toiletries and new socks
