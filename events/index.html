Events

The week, as it actually happens.

Breakfast is the heartbeat. Training, meal packing, and a seasonal gathering fill in the rest. RSVP so we know how many chairs — and so we can find you if the kitchen moves.

Weekly

Recovery Breakfast

Tuesday–Saturday, 10:00–11:00am · Cape Island Cafe, Falmouth

A free breakfast for the recovery community with a coach in the room. The cafe itself is open 7am–2pm, Tuesday through Saturday. Pull up a chair. No intake paperwork at the door.

RSVP — Recovery Breakfast

No account needed. Your note goes straight to the Root to Rise team. In a crisis, call or text 988.

Training

Recovery Coach training

Monthly classes — next cohort forming · Mashpee & Falmouth

A practical course for people with lived experience who want to coach others. Ethics, listening, resource navigation, and how to stay in your own lane. Ongoing training events follow the first class.

RSVP — Recovery Coach training

No account needed. Your note goes straight to the Root to Rise team. In a crisis, call or text 988.

Volunteer

Community meal pack

Select weeknights · Falmouth kitchen

We cook and pack meals for neighbors who cannot make breakfast. Volunteers are the whole engine. Bring an apron if you have one; we will hand you a spoon if you do not.

RSVP — Community meal pack

No account needed. Your note goes straight to the Root to Rise team. In a crisis, call or text 988.

Root to Rise

Mashpee & Falmouth, Massachusetts

Formerly Inspiration is Everywhere. A recovery community offering coaching, meals, work, and a way through — on the Upper Cape, in two towns that still look after their own.
