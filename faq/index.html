FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What Root to Rise is, when breakfast happens, how coaching works, and how to help. If it is an emergency, call 988 or 911.

What is Root to Rise?

Root to Rise is a recovery community in Mashpee and Falmouth, Massachusetts. We walk with people in recovery on Cape Cod — offering coaching, meals, work, and a clear path to the resources that make a life possible. We were formerly called Inspiration is Everywhere.

When and where is the Recovery Breakfast?

The Recovery Breakfast meets at Cape Island Cafe, 316 Gifford Street in Falmouth, Tuesday through Saturday, 10:00–11:00am. The cafe itself is open 7am–2pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Walk in and ask for Root to Rise — you will not need a form.

Is recovery coaching free?

Yes — at the Recovery Breakfast, recovery coaching is free and on site, no appointment needed. Coaches can also help you find a meeting, a clinic, a job, or another meal. To ask about coaching, just come to breakfast or send us a message.

Do I need a referral or paperwork to come?

No. No ID, no referral, no intake paperwork at the door. Come as you are. Eat. Talk, or don't. If you want a coach, one is already in the room.

How can I volunteer?

We need hands at the breakfast table, in the kitchen packing meals, with job mentoring (mock interviews, resume help, a ride to a first shift), and helping someone call a clinic, a pantry, or a landlord. Fill out the volunteer form — no account needed — and someone from Root to Rise will write you.

How do I become a recovery coach?

Root to Rise hosts classes and ongoing training for recovery coaches — listening, ethics, resource navigation, and how to show up without taking over. Classes are monthly and the next cohort is forming. Send us a message to ask about it.

How can I donate?

Give one time or monthly on the Give page, and direct your gift to breakfast, meals, coaching, work, or coach training. We also collect in-kind items — shelf-stable breakfast foods and coffee, grocery gift cards, work boots and interview clothes, bus passes and gas cards, toiletries and new socks. Drop-off can be arranged through the contact form.

What if I or someone I love is in crisis?

Call or text 988 — the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — any time, 24/7. If it is an emergency, call 911. You are not alone.

Where does Root to Rise serve?

We serve Mashpee and Falmouth, Massachusetts, on the Upper Cape. The most reliable place to find us in person is the Recovery Breakfast at Cape Island Cafe in Falmouth.

How do I contact Root to Rise?

Use the contact form — a real person writes back. Or find us in person at the Recovery Breakfast, Cape Island Cafe, 316 Gifford Street, Falmouth, Tue–Sat 10–11am.
