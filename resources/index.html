Resources

A map of help on the Cape.

Treatment, meetings, food, work, housing, health — the Cape already has more than most people can hold in their head on a hard day. We keep a list. We will also sit down and walk it with you.

Root to Rise

Mashpee & Falmouth, Massachusetts

A program of Inspiration Is Everywhere Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. A recovery community offering peer recovery coaching, practical help, and a way through — on the Upper Cape, in two towns that still look after their own.
