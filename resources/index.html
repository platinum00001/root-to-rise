Resources

A map of help on the Cape.

Treatment, meetings, food, work, housing, health — the Cape already has more than most people can hold in their head on a hard day. We keep a list. We will also sit down and walk it with you.

Ask the guide

Not clinical advice. A Cape-literate starting point. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 first.

    988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

    Nationwide

    24/7 call, text, or chat if you or someone you love is in crisis.

    Call or text 988

    Massachusetts Substance Use Helpline

    Massachusetts

    Free, confidential help finding treatment, recovery support, and the next right call.

    800-327-5050

    SAMHSA National Helpline

    Nationwide

    Treatment referral and information service, 24/7, in English and Spanish.

    1-800-662-HELP (4357)

    duffyhealthcenter.org
